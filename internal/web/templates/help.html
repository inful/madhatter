Getting Started

The dashboard is your home base. It shows your status for today, your next HAT day, your next WFH day, and upcoming leave across the team.

If your HAT day was swapped, you'll see a swap icon next to your name with a tooltip explaining what happened.

Today

Your status badge tells you where you are today:

  • On-site — you're in the office.
  • WFH — you're working from home.
  • On leave — you reported leave for today.
  • @conference — you're away for a conference (a leave variant).

Weekends and holidays

On a weekend or configured holiday the card shows "Weekend" or "Holiday: " with an Off tag, and the WFH override buttons hide (there's nothing to override on a non-business day).

"Coming in instead"

If you're projected as WFH but you're actually in the office, this button withdraws today's row and flips the badge to On-site. Use it when plans change day-of.

If you know you'll be in the office on a future WFH day, use the "I'll be in on [date]" picker below. It only lists dates you actually have a row for, so submitting the form always withdraws the right row.

"WFH today" for unforeseen circumstances

If you need to report WFH for today (you're sick but working from home, for example), the "WFH today" entry point in the Quick Actions menu creates and settles the request inline. You'll see the outcome immediately — no waiting for the next tick. If today's on-site count has already hit the floor, the report is denied rather than auto-approved.

WFH: requesting and withdrawing

Use WFH to submit work-from-home requests. The settlement scheduler approves or denies each request against the on-site coverage floor.

Your quota

Each member has a cap on WFH days per period. Ad-hoc requests and recurring weekdays count together. When your quota is full, the form blocks new requests until you withdraw an approved one.

The Request WFH form shows your quota for the period containing the date you pick — not always today's period. If you've used 2 of 2 this month, you still see 2 remaining when picking a date next month.

When a request gets denied

A request is denied only when approving it would drop the on-site headcount below the minimum. Every denial carries a human-readable reason — visible under the Denied tag on your WFH list, on the admin manage page, and in the email notification. You never see a bare "Denied" without context.

Recurring weekdays

If you have a weekday set as recurring WFH, every upcoming occurrence in the current period is treated as an approved request. Withdrawing a single occurrence frees one quota slot; the next period reverts to the configured schedule.

If you self-withdraw a recurring occurrence and then re-request the same date, the new request is treated as ad-hoc and goes through normal settlement — so it may be denied if on-site coverage is at the floor.

Withdrawing

You can withdraw an approved request any time before the WFH day itself. The button is labelled "Coming in instead" for recurring rows and "Withdraw" for ad-hoc rows — the action is the same.

You can also cancel a pending request (before settlement) from your WFH list. If you change your mind afterwards you can re-request the same date.

Leave

Use Leave to report absences. The system assigns a cover automatically.

If a colleague calls in sick, any authenticated user can register that absence on their behalf via the "Someone Called In Sick" form (in the user menu under Leave). The date is pinned to today; duplicates for the same person on the same day are refused.

Each leave record carries a type tag (Leave or Conference). Conference leave is the same as Leave except for the dashboard badge — it shows "@conference" instead of "On leave" and uses a different icon. The tag is purely cosmetic.

Calendar Subscriptions

Open Calendar to create subscription links for your rota and meetings feeds.

Your feed includes your HAT days, your WFH days, your leave, and your teammates' birthdays (so you don't miss the cake). WFH days surface as separate all-day events alongside the HAT assignments; birthdays recur every year on the calendar client.

HAT Day Swaps

Use Swaps to request exchanging your HAT day with another teammate.

Incoming requests can be accepted or rejected. Expired requests are automatically cleaned up and cannot be accepted.

You can cancel your own pending requests before they are accepted.

How HAT Days Are Assigned

Every business day gets exactly one HAT day — the person on call that day. Two independent rotations run side by side, so a leave on one day never reshuffles the rest of the rota.

The original-HAT rotation walks the active team alphabetically, wrapping at the end. The cover rotation is deliberately separate, so a long leave on one person can't freeze the cover slot on another. Whichever member sits at the cover index is the cover candidate; if they're also on leave the system advances cyclically and picks the first one who isn't. Covers never collide with active leave records.

This guarantees a fair distribution: the same person isn't picked for every new leave, and across a year of random leave the total HAT-day count comes out close to equal for every team member.

Need admin help? Contact your rota administrator for team management, policy changes, and access questions.
